About GenLink

GenLink is a powerful genealogy research application that helps users search historical records, maintain detailed research diaries, link to FamilySearch and other major genealogy resources, and organize family history findings across the world.

🔍 Multi-Source Search

Search Google, DuckDuckGo, FamilySearch, Ancestry, and more from one place.

📖 Research Diary

Persistent diary with search, timestamps, and source tracking.

🌍 International Support

Multi-language support for users worldwide.
